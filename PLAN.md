## Translation rules

1. **Language**: Translate Japanese → Vietnamese. Keep proper nouns
   (people, places, brands) in their original form or with romanization
   in parentheses on first use.
   - Example: 富山県 → tỉnh Toyama (富山県)
   - Example: 公文式 → Kumon (公文式)

2. **Tone**: The original is a personal, dramatic first-person narrative.
   Preserve that tone. Do NOT sanitize strong language or emotions.

3. **Structure**: Keep the exact Markdown structure intact:
   - Heading levels (`#`, `##`, `###`) unchanged
   - Image links `![...](https://...)` unchanged - do NOT translate URLs
   - Tables unchanged in structure - translate cell content only
   - Blockquotes, bold, italic markers preserved
   - LaTeX math (`$...$`, `$$...$$`) unchanged

4. **Technical terms**: When academic/medical terms appear, translate to
   Vietnamese and keep the Japanese/English term in parentheses on first use.
   - ASD (自閉スペクトラム症) → rối loạn phổ tự kỷ (ASD / 自閉スペクトラム症)
   - ギフテッド (Gifted) → người có năng khiếu bẩm sinh (gifted)
   - 統合失調症 → tâm thần phân liệt (統合失調症)

5. **Furigana / ruby**: The original uses furigana like `文系ぶんけい` (small
   reading after the kanji). Just translate the concept - drop the furigana.

6. **Length**: Do not summarize. Translate everything faithfully.
   If the source paragraph has 5 sentences, the translation should also
   have roughly 5 sentences.

7. **Physics / math sections**: Chapter 1 has physics content
   (E=mc², Maxwell's demon, wave-particle duality). Translate the
   explanations but keep formulas, variable names, and units unchanged.

## Suggested workflow for the AI

For each chapter:

1. Read `raw/chapter-N.md`
2. Identify all `## ` sections
3. Translate section-by-section, writing incrementally to
   `chuong-N-...md`
4. After finishing, do a pass to check:
   - All images still present
   - All headings match structure
   - No section skipped
   - Vietnamese reads naturally, not like word-by-word MT output

## What NOT to do

- Do NOT re-fetch from the internet - use the local files
- Do NOT summarize or paraphrase heavily - full translation
- Do NOT skip the tables, math, or citations
- Do NOT change image URLs
- Do NOT add your own commentary or footnotes
- Do NOT translate proper nouns to Vietnamese equivalents (keep original)
